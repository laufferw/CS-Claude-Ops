# Playbook: Expansion

> Generic pattern. Adapt the handoff to how your Sales and CS teams split expansion.

## Purpose

Turn expansion signals into qualified opportunities, at the right time, without putting a healthy relationship at risk.

## Trigger

An expansion signal on an account, such as:

- The customer asks about more seats, products, or teams
- Seat utilization above 90% for 60 days
- A new team, site, or use case appears in meeting notes
- A success plan goal is met early and the customer wants the next one

The [Weekly CSM Brief](../prompts/weekly-csm-brief.md) lists these in its expansion section.

## Inputs

- The signal, with its source and date
- Health band and drivers
- Success plan outcomes, as the evidence of value
- Renewal date and current contract terms

## Qualify first

An expansion signal is worth pursuing only if all of these are true:

| Check | Why |
|---|---|
| Band is Green or Yellow | Selling into an unhappy account damages the base renewal |
| No open escalation | Fix what is broken first |
| At least one success plan goal met, with evidence | Expansion needs a value story |
| The customer has named a need, not just shown usage | Usage alone is a hypothesis |

If any check fails, log the signal and revisit it when the account qualifies.

## Steps

| Step | Owner | Timing |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Qualify the signal against the checks above | CSM | Within 5 business days of the signal |
| 2. Discovery: the need, who owns the budget, and the timeline | CSM | Within 2 weeks |
| 3. Hand off to Sales with the need, the value evidence, and the stakeholders | CSM to Sales | When discovery is complete |
| 4. Proposal and pricing | Sales, Finance | Per the sales process |
| 5. Onboard the expansion: update the success plan with goals for the new scope | CSM | After signature (follow [Onboarding](onboarding.md) for a new team or product) |

## Decision logic

- **Renewal within 120 days:** decide with Sales whether to fold expansion into the renewal or keep it separate. Never let expansion delay the base renewal.
- **Signal comes from a non-decision-maker:** find out who owns the budget before any proposal.
- **Customer asks for pricing directly:** the CSM introduces Sales. The CSM does not quote.

## Outputs

- Each signal logged with its date and qualification result
- Qualified opportunities handed to Sales with discovery notes
- An updated success plan for any expansion that closes

## Failure modes

- Pitching expansion to a Yellow account with an unresolved problem. The qualification checks prevent this.
- Treating high usage as a buying signal without a stated need.
- Expansion that closes without new success plan goals, so the new scope has nothing to measure at the next renewal.
- The CSM is seen as a salesperson, and the customer stops being candid about problems.
